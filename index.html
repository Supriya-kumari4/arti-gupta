About Arti

A warm, professional space to understand what you're going through.

Arti Gupta is a Clinical Hypnotherapist, Rehabilitation Psychologist and trauma-focused therapist based in Bhopal. She is also associated with Gandhi Medical College as a Psychologist.

Her work is centered around creating a safe and respectful space where people can talk, reflect and work towards healthier ways of coping.

What are the different types of psychological counselling therapies?

The different types of psychological counselling therapies include behavioural therapy, psychoanalytic therapy, artistic therapy, music therapy, etc.

How can I book an appointment at Samadhan Counseling Clinic?

You can call the clinic on 08460377437 for appointments and enquiries.

Can psychologists prescribe medication?

No, psychologists cannot prescribe medicines. They diagnose and treat patients through therapy.

Does the clinic charge patients on a per-session basis?

Yes, most psychological services charge per session. Please contact the clinic for current session details.

How long does a counselling session last?

A counselling session can vary depending on how much the patient has to discuss with the psychologist.

Does the clinic cater to both men and women?

Yes, the clinic caters to both men and women. You can contact the clinic for further details.
